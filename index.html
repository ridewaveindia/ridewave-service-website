Frequently Asked

Honest answers about how we engineer, deliver, and partner.

What does an "AI-driven digital transformation" engagement look like?

It starts with a discovery sprint — we map your business outcomes, current systems, and data assets, then propose where AI, automation, and cloud-native engineering create the highest leverage. From there, we deliver in iterative milestones, with measurable outcomes at each stage.

Which industries do you specialize in?

Healthcare, finance, ecommerce, logistics, education, manufacturing, real estate, and government. Our engineering practices are particularly suited to regulated and high-scale environments where compliance, latency, and reliability matter.

How do you approach security and compliance?

Security is engineered in from day one. We build to PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and SOX-ready standards by default, applying threat modelling, secret management, encryption at rest and in transit, and zero-trust principles at every layer.

Can you handle small startups as well as enterprise modernization?

Yes. We work with early-stage SaaS startups all the way through to enterprise modernization programs. The same engineering principles — scalable, secure, observable, automated — apply at every size; we right-size the team and stack to your stage and budget.

What technologies do you typically use?

Python (FastAPI / Django) and Node for services, React for frontends, Postgres / Kafka / Redis for the data layer, Docker & Kubernetes orchestration, Terraform for infrastructure, and AWS / Azure / GCP at the cloud layer — paired with the right LLM, vector DB, and ML tooling for AI workloads.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused MVP can ship in 4–8 weeks. A platform build typically runs 3–6 months. Enterprise modernization programs run in quarters, with continuous value delivered at every milestone — no big-bang launches.
